# Security policy

This policy applies to every NexLogin repository that doesn't have its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hello@nexlogin.io** with "Security" in the subject. Please include:

- what is affected (repository, package version, or nexlogin.io page/API),
- steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed,
- whether you'd like to be credited.

Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability, and don't access other people's accounts or data while testing.

We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you updated until it's resolved.

## Scope

In scope: nexlogin.io, api.nexlogin.io, and the code in this organization's repositories.
Out of scope: denial-of-service, spam or social-engineering tests, and findings that require a compromised device.
